We are building two things. A data layer for agents. And the agents themselves.

Businesses don't run on rows. They run on shared meaning, enforced by humans, across a dozen disagreeing systems. tend replaces that labor with a standardized data layer agents can actually read, plus agents that ship the work nobody on your team wants to do.

The last decade gave us better rails. Warehouses, pipelines, reverse-ETL.

None of it agreed on what an account was. Or a renewal. Or a ticket.

So humans kept doing the chasing, routing, and reconciling between systems.

The next decade needs two things. A layer agents can act on. And agents that take the busywork off the team's plate.

We are building both.

P01

Types over rows

A business is made of entities, not tables. We model what things are, not where they sit.

P02

Governance first

If an agent can do it, a policy can bound it. No ungoverned writes, no shadow automations.

P03

Lineage always

Every fact in the graph points back to its source. Every action points back to its actor.

P04

No magic defaults

The platform doesn't guess. Your team writes the rules; we make them legible and testable.

P05

Boring in production

Agents that surprise you in staging shouldn't surprise you in prod. Replay, retry, observe.

P06

Customer telemetry, not trust

You see every token, every tool-call, every write. We ship the dashboards that prove it.
