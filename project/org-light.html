Four stages.

Each one earns the next. Skip ahead and the company stalls.

01

Readiness

≈ 30 days

We map the company twice. Systems: where information lives, where work bottlenecks, where AI changes the work. People: how leadership decides, who carries weight the org chart doesn't show.

You leave with a readiness report and the scaffolding of the data layer that everything we build next runs on.

Why this first You can't transition a company you haven't seen. Most AI projects skip this. Most fail by week six.
02

Thinking Partner

≈ 15 days, then ongoing

An AI thinking partner for the principal. One for each leader if there's a team. Tuned to the person, fed the audit from Stage 1, configured around how they actually decide.

Existing systems untouched. The partner runs in parallel. Leadership starts thinking through it before anyone else feels the change.

Why this second A company can't move together if its leadership doesn't see together.
03

Company Workspace

≈ 45 days

The pattern extends to the whole team. One workspace. One memory. The company's brain, accessible to everyone.

anticipatory workspace
passive tools autonomous agents

A tool waits to be told. An agent runs without you. The workspace sits between. It anticipates, surfaces, proposes. The team stays in charge.

Why this third Only a team that trusts the direction can absorb a shared surface without splitting.
04

Custom System

≈ 60 to 120 days

The final layer. Custom software. Deep automation. Full integration with the systems you already run.

By here the team is fluent. The audit told us what to build. We build the system around the work, not the other way round.

Why this fourth Built first, it overwhelms the company. Built fourth, it transforms it.
